Pours

Match fills honestly at the start of a round. Uneven pours are where every flip cup argument comes from, and they're how someone ends up drinking twice what they signed up for.

Outs

Anybody can sub a water or tap out of a chug without explaining themselves. A game that needs someone forced into it isn't the game — it's just pressure with a table.

Endings

Driver picked before the first rack. Water between games, not after them. Beer ball and the bus both hit harder than they look on paper — don't stack them back to back.
